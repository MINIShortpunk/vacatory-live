    Type: Vacation Scheme

    Location: United Kingdom: London

    • Applications open: 2026-09-17
    • Closing date: 2026-12-13 23:59:59 Europe/London
    • Application timing: Applications open 17 September 2026 and close at midnight on 13 December 2026. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.
    • Programme dates: 5–16 April 2027.
    • Status: open

    Published application and programme dates

    • Applications open 17 September 2026 and close at midnight on 13 December 2026. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.
    • Programme: 5–16 April 2027.
    • Who can apply: Penultimate-year law students, final-year law and non-law students, and graduates
    • Eligibility: Open to penultimate-year law students graduating in 2028, final-year law and non-law students graduating in 2027, and graduates from any degree discipline. Applicants should be on track for, or have achieved, at least a 2:1 degree.
    • Academic requirements: Applicants should be on track for, or have achieved, at least a 2:1 degree. There are no minimum GCSE or A-level requirements.
    • Application process: Online application, untimed strengths-based assessment, pre-recorded video interview, telephone interview and London assessment centre.
    • Progression: Opportunity to interview for the two-year London Training Programme beginning in September 2029.
    • Funding: Akin offers a benefits package intended to reduce financial barriers; the official page does not publish the vacation-scheme payment amount.

    Pro Bono Scholars Program

    Type: Scholarship / Bursary

    • Application timing: Applications are open in Dallas and Houston. Applications for the other participating offices open on 15 December 2026; closing dates are not published.
    • Programme dates: The 1L summer is split between Akin and a public-interest organisation; scholars return as 2L summer associates.
    • Duration: 10 weeks during the first summer, including time at Akin and a public-interest organisation
    • Status: rolling

    Published application and programme dates

    • Applications are open in Dallas and Houston. Applications for the other participating offices open on 15 December 2026; closing dates are not published.
    • Programme: The 1L summer is split between Akin and a public-interest organisation; scholars return as 2L summer associates.
    • Who can apply: First-year students at ABA-accredited US law schools with an anticipated 2029 graduation date
    • Eligibility: Applicants must be enrolled and in good standing at an ABA-accredited law school and expect to graduate in 2029. Applicants may not hold a similar award from another law firm.
    • Academic requirements: Applicants must be enrolled and in good standing at an ABA-accredited law school.
    • Application process: Apply through the official US law-student application portal and explain how the programme supports your career goals.
    • Progression: Return as a 2L summer associate, with potential progression to a first-year associate role.
    • Funding: Scholars receive the summer associate salary during the 10-week programme and may receive a $50,000 scholarship paid in three stages:
      0,000 on starting as a 1L scholar,
      0,000 on returning as a 2L summer associate, and
    0,000 on accepting an associate offer. View Pro Bono Scholars Program official opportunity page (opens in new tab)
