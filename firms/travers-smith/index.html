Travers Smith

Travers Smith is an independent UK law firm advising listed and private companies, financial institutions, private equity firms and other organisations on major transactions, disputes and advisory matters. The firm operates offices in London and Brussels. Its early-careers recruitment is London-based and includes a Summer Vacation Scheme, a Direct Training Contract route and a Level 7 Solicitor Apprenticeship, alongside events, school work experience, access, mentoring and outreach programmes.

    United Kingdom support

    UK Visa and Funding

    UK qualification funding, maintenance support and published visa-sponsorship or right-to-work information for joining the firm in the United Kingdom.

    UK visa sponsorship and right to work

    Training-contract and vacation-scheme immigration positions are different. The firm holds a sponsor licence and may sponsor eligible Skilled Worker visas for future trainees meeting mandatory requirements. Vacation-scheme participants cannot be sponsored under that licence and must secure immigration permission covering two weeks of paid work.
