    United Kingdom - London - Summer Vacation Scheme

    One of two published routes to a Travers Smith training contract. Participants complete legal work, meet people across the firm and are assessed for a training-contract offer.

    • Location: London
    • Who can apply: Applicants in the second year of any degree or above, including postgraduates and career changers.
    • Programme dates: Two two-week summer schemes normally run in June and July; the next reliable cycle dates are not yet published.
    • Duration: Two weeks
    • Progression: Consideration for a Travers Smith Training Contract.

    View United Kingdom - London - Summer Vacation Scheme official opportunity page (opens in new tab)

    United Kingdom - London - Direct Training Contract

    Direct application route to the Travers Smith Training Contract through the firm’s recruitment-day process.

    • Location: London
    • Who can apply: Law and non-law students, graduates and career changers meeting the published criteria.
    • Programme dates: March 2027 intake; exact start day not published.
    • Duration: Two years
    • Progression: Solicitor qualification, subject to successful completion and regulatory requirements.

    View United Kingdom - London - Direct Training Contract official opportunity page (opens in new tab)

    United Kingdom - London - Level 7 Solicitor Apprenticeship

    Six-year earn-while-you-learn programme combining legal work, academic study, mentoring and professional development.

    • Location: London
    • Who can apply: School leavers who meet the published academic, apprenticeship-funding and right-to-work criteria.
    • Programme dates: Future intakes begin in September; the next reliable application window is not yet published.
    • Duration: Six years
    • Progression: LLB and SQE route leading towards solicitor qualification, subject to completion and regulatory requirements.

    View United Kingdom - London - Level 7 Solicitor Apprenticeship official opportunity page (opens in new tab)
