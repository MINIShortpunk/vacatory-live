    • Application timing: Applications opened on 17 August 2026 and close at 23:59 GMT on 13 November 2026. Training contract offers are made on a rolling basis.

    • Duration: Two years
    • Status: open

    Published application and programme dates

    • Applications opened on 17 August 2026 and close at 23:59 GMT on 13 November 2026. Training contract offers are made on a rolling basis.
    • Programme: September 2029 or March 2030; exact start dates have not yet been published.
    • Applications open in June 2027; exact opening and closing dates are not yet published.
    • Who can apply: Law finalists and graduates.
    • Eligibility: Open to final-year law students, law graduates and postgraduate law applicants. Applicants should demonstrate strong academic performance and an interest in commercial law.
    • Academic requirements: AAB at A level or equivalent; strong 2:1 degree performance, ideally at least 65% in the majority of modules for each completed academic year. Extenuating circumstances are considered.
    • Application process: Online application with full academic breakdown, CV and one-page cover letter Early Careers Recruitment review In-person interview day at the London office One-hour written exercise based on a fictional business Approximately 15 minutes to read and make notes on a current-affairs article Partner interview with two partners, or a partner and senior counsel, including the article discussion Office tour and an unassessed conversation with a current trainee Short HR interview, including reflection on the written exercise and partner interview Offer decision; personal feedback is available to unsuccessful interview candidates
    • Assessments: Written exercise: one hour to review around five or six pages about a fictional business and type a report on a supplied laptop; no legal knowledge or advance preparation is required. It assesses written communication, persuasion, judgement, problem analysis, innovative ideas and commercial knowledge. Article discussion: approximately 15 minutes to read a current-affairs article and make notes before summarising it, giving and defending an opinion, considering counterarguments and adapting where appropriate. It is not a comprehension test or an assessment of political views. Partner interview: conducted by two partners, or a partner and senior counsel. It assesses critical thinking, reasoned argument, motivation for commercial law and Slaughter and May, resilience, influence and interpersonal skills, and may cover the applicant's CV, academic choices, interests and understanding of business and the lawyer's role. HR interview: a short discussion with Recruitment about the applicant's reflections, CV and motivation, with time for questions.
    • Progression: Qualification as a solicitor of England and Wales; most trainees remain with the firm on qualification.
    • Funding: The firm pays PGDL and CCP course fees, provides maintenance grants of £12,000 for the PGDL and £20,000 for the CCP, and offers interest-free joining and hardship loans of up to £3,000.
    • Expenses: Reasonable travel expenses for attending the interview are reimbursed, and funds can be provided in advance where needed.
    • Travel support: Reasonable interview travel costs are reimbursed; advance payment can be requested where financial constraints would otherwise prevent attendance.
    • Right to work: Offers are subject to the legal right to work in the UK. Where required, the firm provides Skilled Worker sponsorship and immigration-adviser support.
    • Disability support: Recruitment-process adjustments are available for applicants with a disability or long-term health condition. Applicants should use the contact details on the official interview page to request support.
