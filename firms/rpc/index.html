    United Kingdom - London Training Contract

    Type: Training Contract

    Location: United Kingdom: London

    • Applications open: 2027-03-15
    • Closing date: 2027-06-21 Europe/London
    • Application timing: Applications open 15 March 2027 and close 21 June 2027.
    • Programme dates: Two-year training contract starting in 2029.
    • Duration: Two years
    • Places: 13 per intake
    • Status: upcoming

    Published application and programme dates

    • Applications open 15 March 2027 and close 21 June 2027.
    • Programme: Two-year training contract starting in 2029.
    • Who can apply: Law and non-law students and graduates.
    • Eligibility: Law students may apply in their penultimate or final year, non-law students in their final year, and graduates at any time.
    • Application process: Complete the online application and RPC early-talent assessment. Shortlisted candidates attend an in-person assessment day in August.
    • Assessments: Online behavioural, cognitive and motivational exercises, followed by an in-person assessment day.
    • Progression: Solicitor qualification in England and Wales.
    • Funding: RPC funds the PGDL where required, the Masters in Legal Practice and SQE examinations, with maintenance grants of up to £12,000.
    • Right to work: RPC can provide Skilled Worker sponsorship after an eligible UK training-contract offer is accepted.
    • Disability support: RPC states that recruitment is inclusive and accessible. Candidates may request reasonable adjustments at any stage, and RPC will work with them to support a fair process.
    View United Kingdom - London Training Contract official opportunity page (opens in new tab)
