Simmons & Simmons

Simmons & Simmons is an international law firm headquartered in London, with offices across Europe, Asia, the Middle East and the United States. Its work is organised around highly regulated sectors including asset management and investment funds, financial institutions, healthcare and life sciences, technology, media and telecommunications, and energy, natural resources, infrastructure and construction.
