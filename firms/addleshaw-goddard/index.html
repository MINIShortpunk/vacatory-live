Addleshaw Goddard

Addleshaw Goddard is an international law firm with more than 1,800 lawyers working across 22 offices in 13 countries in Europe, the Middle East and Asia. Vacatory records each current office separately and distinguishes student recruitment by jurisdiction. The early-careers inventory includes England, Scotland and Ireland placement and qualification routes, the Office of the General Counsel route, school and university insight programmes, five school-leaver apprenticeship types, a Graduate Solicitor Apprenticeship, legal innovation and business-services graduate schemes, Paris trainee opportunities, the Legal Explorer social-mobility programme, the SMART Legal Commitment Grant, BPP Legal Access, 10kBI and Ethnicity.Talent.Law.
