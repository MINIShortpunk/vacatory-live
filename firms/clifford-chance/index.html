    United Kingdom - SPARK

    Type: Development Programme

    Location: United Kingdom: London

    • Applications open: 2026-12-02
    • Closing date: 2027-02-08 Europe/London
    • Application timing: Applications open on 2 December 2026 and close on 8 February 2027. Applications are not reviewed on a rolling basis.
    • Programme dates: Programme Launch: 5 May 2027. Insights and Skills Programme: week commencing 28 June 2027. Shadowing Experience: week commencing 5 July or 12 July 2027.
    • Duration: Nine months across two academic years
    • Status: upcoming

    Published application and programme dates

    • Applications open on 2 December 2026 and close on 8 February 2027. Applications are not reviewed on a rolling basis.
    • Programme: Programme Launch: 5 May 2027. Insights and Skills Programme: week commencing 28 June 2027. Shadowing Experience: week commencing 5 July or 12 July 2027.
    • Who can apply: Early-stage university students seeking experience of commercial law and a possible route to a London Training Contract interview.
    • Eligibility: Applicants must be studying their first undergraduate degree at a UK university and be eligible to work in the United Kingdom.
    • Application process: Complete the online application form, Clifford Chance Immersive Online Assessment, Watson Glaser test and pre-recorded video interview, followed by an in-person London Assessment Day if shortlisted.
    • Assessments: Assessment dates are during the weeks commencing 15 and 22 March 2027.
    • Progression: Successful participants are invited to interview for a Clifford Chance Training Contract at the end of the programme.
    • Funding: Paid elements are £110 per day. The one-week Shadowing Experience pays £550.
    View United Kingdom - SPARK official opportunity page (opens in new tab)

    United Kingdom support

    UK Visa and Funding

    UK qualification funding, maintenance support and published visa-sponsorship or right-to-work information for joining the firm in the United Kingdom.

    UK visa sponsorship and right to work

    London Training Contract: Skilled Worker guidance and a Certificate of Sponsorship are published; the University of Law sponsors Student visas for PGDL/SQE study and Clifford Chance publishes an option to reimburse Student visa fees. SPARK and ACCESS require UK right to work. The Newcastle apprenticeship requires existing UK right to work and does not publish sponsorship. The Middle East Training Contract states that Middle East right to work is not required to apply. No universal sponsorship promise is applied to routes that do not publish one.
