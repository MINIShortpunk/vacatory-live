DLA Piper

DLA Piper is a global law firm operating through separate legal entities. Its careers site describes a network of over 90 offices in more than 40 countries. The current official locations directory contains 89 named DLA Piper office-city entries across 43 countries and territories; independent DLA Piper Africa firms, the Oman relationship firm and regional groups are kept outside the office count.
