    United Kingdom - London Spring Vacation Schemes 2027

    Type: Vacation Scheme

    Location: United Kingdom: London

    • Applications open: 2026-10-01
    • Closing date: 2026-12-01
    • Application timing: Applications are open from 1 October to 1 December 2026 and are reviewed on a rolling basis; no closing time is published.
    • Programme dates: Cohorts run 15–25 March and 30 March–9 April 2027.
    • Duration: Two weeks
    • Places: Up to 15 places per cohort
    • Status: open

    Published application and programme dates

    • Applications are open from 1 October to 1 December 2026 and are reviewed on a rolling basis; no closing time is published.
    • Programme: Cohorts run 15–25 March and 30 March–9 April 2027.
    • Who can apply: Penultimate-year law students, plus finalists and graduates from any degree discipline. Non-law penultimate-year students are not eligible.
    • Eligibility: Penultimate-year law students or finalists and graduates from any degree discipline. Non-law penultimate-year students are not eligible.
    • Academic requirements: Minimum AAB at A level or equivalent and a 2:1 degree obtained or predicted, considered contextually.
    • Application process: Online application, situational judgement assessment, video interview, case study and partner interview.
    • Progression: All participants are automatically assessed for a training contract.
    • Funding: £750 per week, optional half-salary advance, £50 office-café allowance, and additional financial or exceptional accommodation support where needed.
