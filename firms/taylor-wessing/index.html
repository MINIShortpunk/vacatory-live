Taylor Wessing

Taylor Wessing is an internationally connected law firm focused on technology, innovation and complex regulated industries. From 1 June 2026 it operates through 16 offices across 11 jurisdictions. Its former UK business, including Dubai, and the former Netherlands business together with associated offices in Ireland and Belgium now trade as Winston Taylor under a cooperation agreement.
