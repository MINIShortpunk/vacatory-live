    Areas of work

    Practice areas

    The legal services and capabilities listed by the firm.

    Advisory and regulatory

    A&O Shearman's advisory and regulatory team advises blue-chip corporates, financial institutions and investment funds on complex legal, regulatory and governance issues around the world. Official capabilities listed by the firm include: • Administrative and public law • Antitrust • Artificial intelligence • Business and human rights • Cybersecurity • Data privacy and data protection • Environmental • Family office • Financial services advisory and regulatory • Foreign direct investment and CFIUS • Funds and asset management • Global employment and compensation • Private client • Public company advisory and corporate governance • Restructuring, insolvency and capital solutions • Sanctions and international trade • Tax • White-collar defense and global investigations

    Capital markets

    A&O Shearman describes its global capital markets team as one of the strongest and most sophisticated in the world. The team advises investment banks, asset managers, hedge funds, corporates, foreign private issuers and domestic companies across debt, equity, derivatives, structured finance, securitizations, CLOs and corporate trust and agency work. Official capabilities listed by the firm include: • Asset finance • Corporate trust and agency • Debt capital markets • Derivatives • Emerging growth • Emerging markets finance • Equity capital markets • Financial services advisory and regulatory • Funds and asset management • Fund liquidity solutions • Global employment and compensation • Insurance • Islamic finance • Markets Innovation Group • Private equity • Public company advisory and corporate governance • Real estate • Restructuring, insolvency and capital solutions • Sovereign debt • Structured finance, securitizations and CLOs • Sustainable finance • Tax

    Corporate and M&A

    A&O Shearman advises leading companies, investment funds and financial institutions on corporate and M&A transactions globally. The firm states that its M&A lawyers work with antitrust, regulatory, foreign direct investment, capital markets, finance, tax and disputes teams on major and complex transactions. Official capabilities listed by the firm include: • Administrative and public law • Antitrust • Artificial intelligence • Business and human rights • Complex commercial transactions • Cybersecurity • Data privacy and data protection • Digital assets • Emerging growth • Environmental • Equity capital markets • Financial services advisory and regulatory • Foreign direct investment and CFIUS • Global employment and compensation • Insurance • Intellectual property • M&A, JVs and corporate reorganizations • Private equity • Project development and finance • Public company advisory and corporate governance • Real estate • Restructuring, insolvency and capital solutions • Sanctions and international trade • Tax • Technology transactions

    Disputes

    A&O Shearman's disputes practice covers complex contentious work across litigation, arbitration, investigations and enforcement. The firm presents disputes as one of its core global expertise areas, supporting clients on high-stakes and often cross-border matters. Official capabilities listed by the firm include: • Administrative and public law • Antitrust • Artificial intelligence • Asset tracing and recovery litigation • Business and human rights • Construction disputes • Corporate and commercial litigation • Cybersecurity • Data privacy and data protection • Digital assets • Environmental • Financial services litigation • Global employment and compensation • Intellectual property • International arbitration • Insurance • M&A litigation • Political law • Project development and finance • Public company advisory and corporate governance • Restructuring, insolvency and capital solutions • Sanctions and international trade • Securities litigation and enforcement • Tax • White-collar defense and global investigations

    Finance

    A&O Shearman describes its finance team as one of the strongest and deepest in the world, with lawyers in major markets advising on complex domestic and cross-border finance matters. Official capabilities listed by the firm include: • Asset finance • Derivatives • Emerging growth • Emerging markets finance • Financial services advisory and regulatory • Funds and asset management • Fund liquidity solutions • Global loans and structured debt solutions • Insurance • Islamic finance • Leveraged finance and high yield • Project development and finance • Real estate • Restructuring, insolvency and capital solutions • Sovereign debt • Structured finance, securitizations and CLOs • Sustainable finance • Tax • Trade, commodity and export credit finance

    Legal innovation

    A&O Shearman describes its legal innovation work as combining technology, resourcing and end-to-end solutions to help clients manage risk, reduce cost and work more efficiently. The firm highlights Fuse, its technology incubator, and the Markets Innovation Group as part of this offering. Official capabilities listed by the firm include: • Artificial intelligence • Delivery expertise • Flexible specialist resourcing • Fuse • Markets Innovation Group • Innovation
