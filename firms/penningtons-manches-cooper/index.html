    United Kingdom - Next Trainee Solicitor Recruitment Cycle

    Recurring two-year trainee programme with March and September starts and four six-month seats.

    • Location: London, Basingstoke/Reading, Birmingham, Cambridge, Guildford and Oxford
    • Who can apply: Penultimate or final-year law students, final-year non-law students, postgraduates and career changers.
    • Duration: 24 months
    • Progression: Qualified solicitor

    View United Kingdom - Next Trainee Solicitor Recruitment Cycle official opportunity page (opens in new tab)

    United Kingdom - Direct Training Contract Application

    Direct applications are accepted separately from the Vacation Scheme route.

    • Location: Selected UK offices
    • Who can apply: Applicants unable to attend a Vacation Scheme or who already have sufficient legal experience.
    • Duration: Application route
    • Progression: Trainee Solicitor Programme

    View United Kingdom - Direct Training Contract Application official opportunity page (opens in new tab)

    United Kingdom - Next Vacation Scheme Recruitment Cycle

    One winter and two summer paid one-week schemes are normally offered annually, with 12 places per scheme.

    • Location: Basingstoke, Birmingham, Cambridge, Guildford, London, Oxford and Reading
    • Who can apply: Penultimate or final-year law undergraduates, final-year non-law undergraduates, postgraduates and career changers.
    • Duration: One week
    • Progression: Automatic Trainee Solicitor consideration

    View United Kingdom - Next Vacation Scheme Recruitment Cycle official opportunity page (opens in new tab)

    United Kingdom - Next EMpower Programme

    Paid four-day insight, work shadowing, skills and mentoring programme with Vacation Scheme assessment-centre progression.

    • Location: Firm-designated UK location
    • Who can apply: Black heritage or ethnic minority students meeting the published study-stage criteria.
    • Duration: Four days
    • Progression: Vacation Scheme place

    View United Kingdom - Next EMpower Programme official opportunity page (opens in new tab)

    United Kingdom - Next Limitless Programme

    Five-day London work-experience and skills programme for students from low-income backgrounds.

    • Location: London
    • Who can apply: Year 12 or 13/S6 students from state-funded schools who meet at least one published social-mobility criterion.
    • Duration: Five days
    • Progression: Future education and career applications

    View United Kingdom - Next Limitless Programme official opportunity page (opens in new tab)
