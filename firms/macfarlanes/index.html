Macfarlanes

Macfarlanes is an independent law firm headquartered in London, with a Brussels office and a New York representative office. Its principal strengths include private capital, private wealth, M&A and disputes. The New York office provides market intelligence and does not provide legal services.

    United Kingdom - First Year Insight Scheme

    Type: Insight Programme

    Location: United Kingdom: London

    • Applications open: 2026-11-02
    • Closing date: 2027-01-15
    • Application timing: Applications open 2 November 2026 and close 15 January 2027.
    • Programme dates: 28 June to 2 July 2027.
    • Duration: Five days
    • Places: Approximately 25 places.
    • Status: open

    Published application and programme dates

    • Applications open 2 November 2026 and close 15 January 2027.
    • Programme: 28 June to 2 July 2027.
    • Who can apply: First-year students on three-year degrees and second-year students on four-year degrees, from any degree subject.
    • Eligibility: Open to first-year students on three-year degrees and second-year students on four-year degrees. Law and non-law students may apply.
    • Academic requirements: No minimum A-level grades are specified. Macfarlanes generally looks for applicants who are on course for, or have achieved, a 2:1 degree.
    • Application process: Complete the online application form and the published online assessment and interview stages.
    • Assessments: Online situational judgement and critical-reasoning assessment, followed by a short telephone interview.
    • Progression: The programme provides experience relevant to later vacation-scheme and training-programme applications.
    • Funding: This is a paid programme. Eligible participants may also receive accommodation and work-attire support through the Macfarlanes Bursary.
    • Expenses: Travel expenses are reimbursed for in-person events and assessment days.
    • Travel support: Travel expenses are reimbursed for in-person events and assessment days.
    • Accommodation support: Eligible participants who live outside London may receive accommodation support through the Macfarlanes Bursary.
    • Right to work: Applicants must have the right to work in the UK. Students with a Student visa may participate only where the entire scheme falls outside their university term time.
    • Disability support: Applicants can request reasonable adjustments during the recruitment process.
