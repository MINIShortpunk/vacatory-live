    Published application and programme dates

    • Opened 13 May 2026; closed Sunday 28 June 2026 at 11:59pm.
    • Who can apply: A disabled individual as defined by the Equality Act 2010.
    • Eligibility: Disabled as defined by the Equality Act 2010; evidence required. Must hold an offer for the specified University of Law course.

    Published application and programme dates

    • External entry is through advertised paralegal vacancies. Existing Stewarts paralegals may apply internally from 1 to 31 March each year.
    • Programme: 1 October following the successful internal cycle; exact offer-year linkage is not separately stated.
    • Who can apply: External applicants seeking the only published route into a Stewarts training contract, followed by existing Stewarts paralegals at the internal application stage.
    • Eligibility: internal paralegal only: true minimum service: false
    • Application process: Apply for a paralegal vacancy when advertised. Once employed as a Stewarts paralegal, apply internally between 1 and 31 March.
    • Assessments: In Tray exercise Blind points-based marking Drafting exercise Partner-panel interview
    • Progression: Approximately eight two-year training contracts each year, beginning on 1 October.
    • Disability support: Applicants should contact Stewarts HR about individual reasonable adjustments; no detailed trainee-specific process is published.
