    United Kingdom - London and Leeds - Paralegal roles

    Paid permanent full-time paralegal roles forming the exclusive internal route to Stewarts Training Contracts.

    • Location: London and Leeds
    • Who can apply: Students, graduates and career changers.
    • Duration: Permanent; 37.5 hours per week
    • Progression: Internal Training Contract

    United Kingdom - London and Leeds - Trainee solicitor role

    Litigation-focused two-year Training Contract recruited exclusively from the internal paralegal pool.

    • Location: London and Leeds
    • Who can apply: Existing Stewarts paralegals.
    • Applications: 1 March 2027 to 31 March 2027
    • Programme dates: 1 October following the successful internal cycle; exact offer-year linkage is not separately stated.
    • Duration: 24 months
    • Progression: NQ Associate

    View United Kingdom - London and Leeds - Trainee solicitor role official opportunity page (opens in new tab)

    United Kingdom - London and Leeds - Internal trainee application window

    Annual internal Training Contract application window.

    • Location: London and Leeds
    • Who can apply: All Stewarts paralegals.
    • Applications: 1 March 2027 to 31 March 2027
    • Duration: 1 month
    • Progression: Training Contract offer

    View United Kingdom - London and Leeds - Internal trainee application window official opportunity page (opens in new tab)

    United Kingdom - London and Leeds - Work experience programme

    One-week summer work experience covering legal concepts, legal careers, CVs and interview techniques.

    • Location: London and Leeds
    • Who can apply: 16 and 17-year-olds introduced through the Social Mobility Foundation.
    • Duration: 1 week
    • Progression: Future education and career applications

    View United Kingdom - London and Leeds - Work experience programme official opportunity page (opens in new tab)

    United Kingdom - Daniel Turnbull Memorial Scholarship

    Regular scholarship supporting completion of the LPC at any University of Law campus.

    • Location: United Kingdom
    • Who can apply: A disabled individual as defined by the Equality Act 2010.

    • Progression: Legal career progression
