    Canada - Gowling WLG Life Sciences, Society & the Law Scholarship

    Type: Scholarship / Bursary

    Location: Canada

    • Application timing: Annual scholarship; the next application window has not yet been published.
    • Programme dates: Annual $5,000 award for a Canadian JD/LLB student writing on life sciences, society and the law. The next award-cycle dates are not yet published.
    • Status: variable

    Published application and programme dates

    • Annual scholarship; the next application window has not yet been published.
    • Programme: Annual $5,000 award for a Canadian JD/LLB student writing on life sciences, society and the law. The next award-cycle dates are not yet published.
    • Who can apply: Law students enrolled, or due to be enrolled, in a JD/LLB programme at an accredited Canadian law school who are Canadian citizens or permanent residents and interested in life sciences law.
    • Eligibility: Current or upcoming JD/LLB enrolment at an accredited Canadian law school; Canadian citizen or permanent resident; interest in a legal career in the life sciences sector.
    • Application process: Submit the official application form, a cover letter and the required 1,500-word legal paper when the next annual cycle opens.
    • Funding: $5,000 annual scholarship. The successful applicant is also invited to participate in Gowling WLG’s annual Life Sciences, Society and the Law Symposium.
