Shoosmiths

Shoosmiths is a UK commercial law firm with offices across England, Scotland and Northern Ireland and a Brussels presence. Its early-careers routes include trainee solicitor programmes, vacation placements, solicitor apprenticeships and insight events. Shoosmiths says its emerging-talent programmes are currently being reviewed and redesigned: applications are closed, existing trainee intakes will continue, and further programme details are expected ahead of a launch in Autumn 2027.
