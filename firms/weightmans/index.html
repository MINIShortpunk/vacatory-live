    United Kingdom - Solicitor Degree Apprenticeship

    Type: Apprenticeship

    Location: United Kingdom

    • Application timing: Applications are closed; the apprenticeship hub says recruitment usually opens in November and closes in January.
    • Duration: Six years
    • Status: closed

    Published application and programme dates

    • Applications are closed; the apprenticeship hub says recruitment usually opens in November and closes in January.
    • Who can apply: School leavers eligible for current Level 7 apprenticeship funding.
    • Eligibility: Aged 16–21 at the start, or under 25 and either a care leaver or holder of an Education, Health and Care Plan.
    • Academic requirements: 128 UCAS points and at least five GCSEs at grade 4/C or above, including English and Mathematics.
    • Application process: Written application, interactive online assessment, virtual interview and assessment centre.
    • Assessments: The published 2026 process used January virtual interviews and February assessment centres.
    • Progression: Qualification as a solicitor.
    • Funding: Degree and SQE study funded. Salary starts at £22,932 (£25,207 in London), rising to £31,000 (£38,000 in London) by the final year.
