    Type: Work Experience

    Location: United Kingdom

    • Duration: Four hours across two online sessions
    • Status: unknown

    Published application and programme dates

    • An expression-of-interest form is available. Full registration dates are not published.

    United Kingdom - Solicitor Degree Apprenticeship

    Type: Apprenticeship

    Location: United Kingdom

    • Closing date: 2026-10-22 12:00:00 Europe/London
    • Application timing: Applications are open and close at noon on 22 October 2026.
    • Programme dates: September 2027 start; exact start day is not published.
    • Duration: Six years
    • Status: open

    Published application and programme dates

    • Applications are open and close at noon on 22 October 2026.
    • Programme: September 2027 start; exact start day is not published.
    • Who can apply: School and college leavers eligible under the current Level 7 apprenticeship funding rules.
    • Eligibility: Applicants must be aged 16 to 21, or under 25 and either a care leaver or have an Education, Health and Care Plan at the start of the programme.
    • Academic requirements: 120 UCAS points and at least five GCSEs at grade 4/C or above, including English and Mathematics.
    • Application process: Written application, HIPO-I online assessment, virtual interview and assessment centre.
    • Assessments: Virtual interviews in the weeks beginning 7 and 14 December 2026; assessment centres in the weeks beginning 11 and 18 January 2027.
    • Progression: Level 7 Solicitor Apprenticeship, LLB, SQE and qualification as a solicitor.
    • Funding: Paid employment with degree and SQE study funded.
    View United Kingdom - Solicitor Degree Apprenticeship official opportunity page (opens in new tab)

    United Kingdom - Paralegal Apprenticeship

    Type: Apprenticeship

    Location: United Kingdom

    • Closing date: 2026-10-22 12:00:00 Europe/London
    • Application timing: Applications are open and close at noon on 22 October 2026.
    • Programme dates: September 2027 start; exact start day is not published.
    • Duration: Two years
    • Status: open

    Published application and programme dates

    • Applications are open and close at noon on 22 October 2026.
    • Programme: September 2027 start; exact start day is not published.
    • Who can apply: School and college leavers meeting the published academic requirements.
    • Eligibility: The official vacancy does not impose the Level 7 age restriction used for the solicitor apprenticeship.
    • Academic requirements: 96 UCAS points and at least five GCSEs at grade 4/C or above, including English and Mathematics.
    • Application process: Written application, HIPO-I online assessment, virtual interview and assessment centre.
    • Assessments: Virtual interviews in the weeks beginning 7 and 14 December 2026; assessment centres in the weeks beginning 11 and 18 January 2027.
    • Progression: Level 3 Paralegal Apprenticeship and Level 4 Certificate of Higher Education in Law and Legal Practice.
    • Funding: Paid employment with apprenticeship study funded.

    UK Visa and Funding

    UK qualification funding, maintenance support and published visa-sponsorship or right-to-work information for joining the firm in the United Kingdom.

    UK visa sponsorship and right to work

    Sponsorship is possible for the Trainee Solicitor Programme, subject to Home Office Skilled Worker criteria — candidates must either already have the right to work or meet those criteria. Explicitly does NOT sponsor the Summer Vacation Scheme, which requires pre-existing right to work. (Source: weightmans.com official careers pages)
