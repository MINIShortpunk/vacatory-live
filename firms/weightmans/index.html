    United Kingdom - Trainee Solicitor Jobs - SQE pathway

    Fully funded SQE1 and SQE2 preparation with BPP followed by two years of QWE across four seats.

    • Location: National UK offices
    • Who can apply: Law-degree students from second year and final-year non-law students.
    • Duration: SQE preparation plus 24 months QWE
    • Progression: Qualified solicitor

    United Kingdom - Training Contract

    Two-year training contract across four six-month seats for candidates already on the LPC pathway.

    • Location: National UK offices
    • Who can apply: Graduates studying toward or who have completed the LPC.
    • Duration: 24 months
    • Progression: Qualified solicitor
