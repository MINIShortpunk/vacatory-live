Herbert Smith Freehills Kramer

Herbert Smith Freehills Kramer, known as HSF Kramer, is an integrated global law firm created by the combination of Herbert Smith Freehills and Kramer Levin on 1 June 2025. The firm officially reports approximately 2,700 lawyers, including around 630 partners, across 26 offices. Its capabilities cover complex transactions, disputes, regulation, restructuring, private capital, real estate, technology and sector-focused work.
