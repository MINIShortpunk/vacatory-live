Dechert is an international law firm with offices across the United States, Europe, Asia and the Middle East. Its current student pathways include London vacation schemes, a Dublin internship, U.S. summer associates, Paris law-clerk internships and a Luxembourg trainee programme.

    Ireland - Dublin Summer Internship 2027

    Type: Internship

    Location: Ireland: Dublin

    • Applications open: 2026-12-01
    • Closing date: 2027-02-12 Europe/Dublin
    • Application timing: Applications open 1 December 2026 and close 12 February 2027; no closing time is published.
    • Programme dates: July 2027; exact dates are agreed with successful candidates.
    • Duration: Four weeks
    • Status: upcoming

    Published application and programme dates

    • Applications open 1 December 2026 and close 12 February 2027; no closing time is published.
    • Programme: July 2027; exact dates are agreed with successful candidates.
    • Who can apply: Students who will have completed at least two years of any degree by summer 2027, postgraduates and graduates from any discipline.
    • Eligibility: Open across degree disciplines to applicants meeting the stated study-stage requirement.
    • Academic requirements: Applicants are generally expected to be on course for, or have achieved, a 2:1 or first; mitigating circumstances are considered.
    • Application process: Online application; critical reasoning test and written exercise; 15-minute telephone interview; Dublin partner interview and trainee-led office tour.
    • Assessments: Critical reasoning test, written exercise, telephone interview and partner interview.
    • Funding: Paid internship; the official page does not publish the amount. Dechert pays PPC fees for appointed Dublin trainees.
    • Expenses: Up to €100 of interview travel expenses is reimbursed.
    • Disability support: Recruitment adjustments are available through graduate.recruitment@dechert.com.

    Luxembourg Trainee Programme

    Type: Traineeship

    • Application timing: Applications follow live vacancies; no universal deadline is published.
    • Programme dates: Start dates depend on live vacancies and candidate eligibility.
    • Status: rolling

    Published application and programme dates

    • Applications follow live vacancies; no universal deadline is published.
    • Programme: Start dates depend on live vacancies and candidate eligibility.
    • Who can apply: Applicants with a master’s degree in law or equivalent who have passed, or are completing, the CCDL examination.
    • Eligibility: Applicants normally must have passed the CCDL examination for admission to the Luxembourg Bar; candidates completing CCDL may be considered ad hoc.
    • Academic requirements: Master’s degree in law or equivalent; English and French fluency. Other major languages are an asset.
    • Application process: Apply to a live Luxembourg trainee vacancy; HR interview followed by partner interview.
    • Assessments: HR interview and partner interview.

    United Kingdom - London Spring Vacation Scheme 2027

    Type: Vacation Scheme

    Location: United Kingdom: London

    • Closing date: 2026-12-31
    • Application timing: Applications close on 31 December 2026; no closing time is published.
    • Programme dates: 30 March–9 April 2027.
    • Duration: Two weeks
    • Status: open

    Published application and programme dates

    • Applications close on 31 December 2026; no closing time is published.
    • Programme: 30 March–9 April 2027.
    • Who can apply: Penultimate-year law undergraduates; all final-year undergraduates in any discipline; graduates, postgraduates and career changers.
    • Eligibility: Open to eligible applicants from law and non-law backgrounds. Dechert does not accept direct London training-contract applications; trainee solicitors are recruited through its vacation schemes.
    • Academic requirements: Dechert says recruits generally have at least AAB or equivalent and are on course for, or have achieved, a 2:1 or first. Applications are considered holistically and mitigating circumstances may be provided.
    • Application process: Online application; critical reasoning test and written exercise; 15-minute telephone interview; partner and senior-associate interview, followed by an office tour and trainee coffee chat.
    • Assessments: Critical reasoning test, written exercise, telephone interview and final interview.
    • Funding: Vacation-scheme pay is £550 per week. Future trainees offered and accepting before study begins receive PGDL or SQE preparation tuition funding and a £20,000 annual maintenance grant for full-time study.
    • Expenses: Up to £100 of interview travel expenses is reimbursed.
    • Disability support: Recruitment adjustments are available through graduate.recruitment@dechert.com.
    View United Kingdom - London Spring Vacation Scheme 2027 official opportunity page (opens in new tab)
