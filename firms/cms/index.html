    United Kingdom - Scotland Training Contract

    Type: Training Contract

    Location: United Kingdom: Aberdeen, Edinburgh, Glasgow

    • Applications open: 2026-09-24
    • Closing date: 2027-01-10
    • Application timing: Applications opened 24 September 2026 and close 10 January 2027.
    • Programme dates: The training contract includes a two-week CMS Academy before the intake starts.
    • Duration: 2 years
    • Status: open

    Published application and programme dates

    • Applications opened 24 September 2026 and close 10 January 2027.
    • Programme: The training contract includes a two-week CMS Academy before the intake starts.
    • Who can apply: Current Scottish LLB and accelerated LLB students meeting the published study-stage rules.
    • Eligibility: Open to penultimate- or final-year Scottish LLB students and first- or second-year accelerated LLB students.
    • Academic requirements: Penultimate/final-year Scottish LLB or first/second-year accelerated LLB
    • Application process: Online application form Critical-thinking test 15-minute pre-recorded video interview Assessment day
    • Assessments: Critical-thinking test 15-minute pre-recorded video interview Assessment day
    • Progression: Qualified lawyer / associate
    • Funding: CMS sponsors the Diploma in Professional Legal Practice (DPLP) and pays a maintenance grant. The official Scottish page confirms sponsorship and maintenance support but does not publish a Scotland-specific amount.
    • Right to work: general training contract policy: CMS says it will endeavour to support a work-permit application after a Training Contract offer; employment is conditional on work authorisation.
    • Disability support: Applicants may request reasonable adjustments from CMS recruitment for the relevant programme.
    View United Kingdom - Scotland Training Contract official opportunity page (opens in new tab)
