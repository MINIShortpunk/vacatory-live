BCLP

BCLP is a global law firm with 31 offices in nine countries and more than 1,200 lawyers. Vacatory records the firm’s London and Manchester graduate routes, school and university insight, Legal Placement, London–Paris route, eleven official BCLP Forage simulations, the US Summer Associate programme and four office-specific US programme masters. The office-specific US records are retained as genuine routes but marked currently closed with an official-source conflict because BCLP’s main US student hub lists them while its current-opportunities page says no summer or entry-level applications are presently being accepted.
