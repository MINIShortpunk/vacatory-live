    United Kingdom - TLT Vacation Scheme - Scotland

    Type: Vacation Scheme

    Location: United Kingdom: Edinburgh, Glasgow

    • Applications open: 2026-09-28
    • Closing date: 2026-12-20
    • Application timing: Vacation Scheme applications open 28 September 2026 and close 20 December 2026.
    • Programme dates: Paid two-week vacation scheme in June 2027.
    • Duration: 2 weeks
    • Status: open

    Published application and programme dates

    • Vacation Scheme applications open 28 September 2026 and close 20 December 2026.
    • Programme: Paid two-week vacation scheme in June 2027.
    • Who can apply: Law students and law graduates applying for the Scotland vacation scheme.
    • Eligibility: The Scotland vacation scheme is open to law students and law graduates. Applicants select Edinburgh or Glasgow when applying.
    • Academic requirements: A 2:1 degree is required or must be predicted.
    • Application process: Apply online. Shortlisted applicants attend a half-day assessment centre with a group exercise and interview.
    • Assessments: Half-day assessment centre with a group exercise and interview; the scheme also includes a solution-based group task.
    • Progression: Participants are considered for a TLT traineeship starting in 2029; earlier starts may occasionally be available according to business need.
    • Funding: The vacation scheme is paid at a competitive rate; TLT does not publish an exact amount.
    • Right to work: Applicants must have the right to work in the UK for the vacation scheme. Any later traineeship immigration requirements are assessed separately.
    • Disability support: Reasonable adjustments are available during recruitment; requests can be sent to EarlyCareers@tlt.com.

    United Kingdom - TLT Vacation Scheme - England

    Type: Vacation Scheme

    Location: United Kingdom: Bristol, Manchester

    • Applications open: 2026-09-28
    • Closing date: 2026-12-20
    • Application timing: Vacation Scheme applications open 28 September 2026 and close 20 December 2026.
    • Programme dates: Paid two-week vacation scheme in June 2027.
    • Duration: 2 weeks
    • Status: open

    Published application and programme dates

    • Vacation Scheme applications open 28 September 2026 and close 20 December 2026.
    • Programme: Paid two-week vacation scheme in June 2027.
    • Who can apply: Penultimate- and final-year law students, law graduates, and eligible non-law graduates or career changers.
    • Eligibility: Law applicants may be in the penultimate or final year of study or have graduated. Non-law graduates must be enrolled on or have completed the PGDL or an accelerated LLB. Applicants who have completed SQE1 and/or SQE2, or the LPC, may also apply.
    • Academic requirements: A 2:1 degree is required or must be predicted. Applicants who have completed the LPC are also eligible.
    • Application process: Apply online. Shortlisted applicants attend a half-day assessment centre with a group exercise and interview.
    • Assessments: Half-day assessment centre with a group exercise and interview; the scheme also includes a solution-based group task.
    • Progression: Participants are considered for a TLT training contract starting in 2029; earlier starts may occasionally be available according to business need.
    • Funding: The vacation scheme is paid at a competitive rate; TLT does not publish an exact amount.
    • Right to work: Applicants must have the right to work in the UK for the vacation scheme. Training-contract visa sponsorship is considered separately and depends on the applicable immigration criteria and chosen office.
    • Disability support: Reasonable adjustments are available during recruitment; requests can be sent to EarlyCareers@tlt.com.
    View United Kingdom - TLT Vacation Scheme - England official opportunity page (opens in new tab)
